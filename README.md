# 📊 La Floridian Restaurant - Risk Management & Financial Analysis Framework

An end-to-end risk management framework and financial analysis report developed for **La Floridian**, an award-winning family restaurant located in Fort Lauderdale Beach[cite: 1, 2]. This project evaluates internal vulnerabilities, financial exposure, and operational hazards to deliver data-driven mitigation strategies.

---

## 📁 Repository Contents
* **`Kuchipudi_ Last assignmentLa Floridian PPT.pdf`**: The executive presentation detailing the risk heat map, SWOT analysis, loss triangulation, risk strategies, and evaluation plan[cite: 1].
* **`Kuchipudi Kushi_ Last Assignment Risk Management Proposal.pdf`**: The comprehensive proposal and report covering detailed financial ratios, risk treatment approaches (Mitigation, Transfer, Retention), and strategic recommendations[cite: 2].

---

## 🔍 Project Overview & Methodology

### 1. Financial & Ratio Analysis
Identified critical financial vulnerabilities impacting business stability:
* **Prime Cost Ratio (109.1%):** Indicates that $1.09 is spent on ingredients, staff, and service for every $1.00 of sales, resulting in a pre-overhead loss[cite: 2].
* **Debt-to-Equity Ratio (7.94):** Highlights high financial leverage (nearly $8 of debt for every $1 invested)[cite: 2].
* **Gross Profit Ratio (50.8%):** Leaves limited margin to cover labor and daily operating expenses[cite: 2].
* **Current Ratio (0.73):** Reflects insufficient liquid capital to cover immediate obligations due over the coming year[cite: 2].

### 2. Risk Assessment & Heat Map
Categorized operational and environmental hazards using a risk impact matrix:
* **High Risk / High Impact:** Safety hazards, poor vendor management, absence of digitized records, and untrained staff[cite: 1, 2].
* **High Risk / Low Impact:** Understaffed shifts, lack of digital tracking, and peak-hour crowd control[cite: 1, 2].

### 3. Loss Triangulation Analysis
Evaluated 5 years of historical claims/accident data (2018–2022) across development intervals (12 to 60 months) to understand financial exposure over time[cite: 1, 2].

### 4. Risk Treatment Strategies
* **Mitigation Focus:** Implement digitized record-keeping systems and structured hiring frameworks[cite: 2].
* **Transfer Focus:** Secure comprehensive insurance policies for physical damages/natural disasters and outsource staffing to third-party providers[cite: 2].
* **Retention Focus:** Internalize vendor management alignment and operational delegation[cite: 2].

---

## 🚀 Key Recommendations
1. **Lease the Property:** Leverage property assets to generate steady income and optimize operations[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Seasonal Menu Optimization:** Introduce local, seasonal seafood and native produce to boost margins while honoring the restaurant's authentic brand reputation[cite: 1, 2].
